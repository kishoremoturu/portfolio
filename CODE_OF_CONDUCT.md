# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and constructive
- Giving and accepting feedback gracefully
- Prioritizing what is best for the project and community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment or discriminatory language
- Personal attacks or trolling
- Publishing others' private information

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior and may take appropriate action in response to unacceptable behavior.

## Reporting

Report concerns to the repository owner through GitHub private communication.

This document is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
